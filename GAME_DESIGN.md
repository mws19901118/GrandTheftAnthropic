# Grand Theft Anthropic — Game Design Bible

## Pitch
A satirical, English-language, GTA-inspired 2.5D isometric open-world browser game set in fictional **AI Vice City**. The player is Dario Amodei, racing toward an Anthropic IPO while navigating competing AI empires, GPU scarcity, market hype and safety bureaucracy. Tone: neon Vice City, Silicon Valley black humor, playful parody rather than allegations of real criminal activity.

## Cast and themes
- **Dario Amodei / Anthropic** — playable lead, “The Safety Don”; trade-offs among compute, money, hype, and safety.
- **Jensen Huang / NVIDIA** — “The Arms Dealer”; leather jacket, CUDA, Blackwell GPUs, sells to everyone.
- **Alice Huang** — Jensen's recurring companion; always beside Jensen in shared scenes, including yacht ending.
- **Sam Altman / OpenAI** — “The Comeback King”; fictional AGI definition files and boardroom firing/reinstatement jokes.
- **Liang Wenfeng / DeepSeek** — “The Efficiency Ghost”; cost-efficiency and market panic.
- **Elon Musk / xAI** — “The Chaos Merchant”; Grok, rockets, Colossus, social media.
- **Sundar Pichai / Google** — “The Sleeping Giant”; Gemini, TPUs, approval bureaucracy.
- **Mark Zuckerberg / Meta** — “The Open-Weight Warlord”; Llama, smart glasses, AI doubles.
- **THE MARKET** — IPO final boss represented by a giant red candlestick chart.

## Current main campaign (10 stages)
1. The Leather Jacket Tax — collect Blackwell shipment from NVIDIA.
2. Deliver shipment to Anthropic before timer.
3. The Ex-OpenAI Files — retrieve fictional AGI definition PDF.
4. Boardroom Resurrection — timed escape.
5. The $6 Million Question — DeepSeek efficiency choice.
6. The Grokfather — reach xAI Colossus.
7. Timed return from xAI to Anthropic.
8. Too Big to Prompt — Google procurement challenge.
9. The Open-Weight Heist — find real Zuck.
10. The IPO Heist — defeat THE MARKET; continue free roam afterward.

## Systems already present
- On-foot and car movement, boost, E interactions, wanted level, police pursuit and BUSTED overlay with mission retry.
- Cash, GPU/compute, hype, safety. Cash can buy/upgrade three properties; resource output occurs every 45 seconds of active play.
- Blackwell Warehouse ($1,100; GPU production), Research Lab ($850; GPU → hype), Cloud Data Center ($1,450; GPU → cash). Level-2 upgrades available.
- Repeatable instant side gigs on G with cooldown. They are an economic fallback, **not yet physical gameplay**.
- Three timed, three-waypoint property missions: The CUDA Cartel, Benchmark Bandits, Cooling Crisis; unlocked by property ownership.
- Minimap, neon isometric renderer, basic districts, faster road driving and building collision.

## Desired gameplay evolution
- Streets should be useful, not optional; distinct districts, winding roads, highway, bridges, waterfront, industrial docks, parks, suburbs/outskirts, identifiable landmarks.
- Locations should be spaced so driving matters, without boring dead travel time. Waypoints, timers and enemy pursuit must scale with geography.
- Convert “drive to checkpoint + press E” into actual missions with adversaries, hazards, cargo, stealth and tactical decisions.
- Properties should have upgrades, mission chains and viable ROI; avoid economic deadlocks. Optional post-IPO vertical monopoly ending; Jensen/Alice yacht joke.
- Visuals should evolve beyond geometric placeholders toward readable, detailed isometric characters, vehicles, props and animations.

## Ending ideas
- Claude Empire, AGI Merger, secret Jensen Wins Everything / Vertical Monopoly. Only the IPO/free-roam ending and Jensen joke are presently implemented; others are design targets.

## Creative constraints
- Keep the game original and parody-focused, not a copy of GTA art/assets or trademarked UI.
- Public figures are fictional characters in a comic narrative; separate fictional acts from real-world factual claims.
