GRAND THEFT ANTHROPIC v0.8 — PROPERTY WARS
Open index.html in a modern browser. All files must remain together.
WASD/arrows move; E interact / enter vehicle / claim asset checkpoint; Space boost; B portfolio; G repeatable side gig; R restart campaign.

NEW: Purchase a property, approach it and press E, then START its asset mission. Follow the CYAN marker and press E at each of three checkpoints before the timer expires. Police contact can cause BUSTED. The CUDA CARTEL has a wanted level. Complete once for full payout, replay for a smaller payout. The main story is paused during asset contracts (start them outside timed main-story delivery missions). Property income continues on its normal 45-second cycle.

ARCHITECTURE
story.js — main story data
properties.js — property economics and locations
asset_missions.js — side-mission waypoints, scripts, rewards, timers
game.js — movement, mission runtime, drawing, UI
style.css — visual UI

Fictional satire. Real individuals are not accused of fictional in-game acts.
